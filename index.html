A home with purpose

Care that puts the person first.

At House of David AFH, we understand that choosing a care home is about much more than services. It is about trust, comfort, safety and knowing that your loved one is treated with respect.

Our approach combines experienced care with the warmth of a home-like setting, helping residents feel supported while preserving their dignity and individuality.

What we provide

Everyday support, thoughtfully delivered.

Our services are designed around comfort, safety and the practical needs of each resident.

Home-Cooked Meals

Three home-cooked meals served daily with snacks in between meals.

Housekeeping & Laundry

Weekly housekeeping and laundry services, and as needed.

Health Maintenance

Medication assistance under the supervision of a nurse delegator and health monitoring as part of attentive daily care.

Care Coordination

Coordination of appointments and care with your physicians and other medical providers.

Personal Care

Assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, hygiene and everyday routines per resident care plan.

Our story

A vision shaped by decades of caring for others.

Our owners, David and Irene, have both been working in long term care for decades. When they moved to Washington, they brought that education, experience and passion with them.

Their vision was simple: create a place where people could receive quality, long-term care while still experiencing the warmth and familiarity of home.

That vision led to the opening of House of David AFH in Olympia and has since grown to include House of Blessings AFH in Yelm.

Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers for families.

Choosing a care home comes with important questions. Here are a few answers to help you understand House of David AFH and plan your next step.

Ask Us a Question
What is an Adult Family Home?

An Adult Family Home provides personal care and support in a smaller, residential setting. The goal is to combine needed assistance with the comfort and familiarity of a home-like environment.

What everyday services does House of David provide?

Our listed services include home-cooked meals, housekeeping and laundry, personal care, medication assistance, health monitoring, care coordination, behavioral-health support.

What kinds of care needs do you have experience supporting?

Our experience includes dementia care, Parkinson’s disease, diabetes, stroke care, COPD, wound and catheter care, dialysis-related needs, behavioral support and other complex conditions. Every prospective resident is considered individually so we can discuss whether the home is an appropriate fit.

Do you help with medications and healthcare coordination?

Yes. Our services include medication assistance and health monitoring, along with coordination of appointments and care with physicians and other providers.

Can medical providers visit the home?

House of David offers access to a range of visiting and on-site services, including primary and long-term care providers, podiatry, X-ray services, lab services and traveling urgent care. Contact us to discuss current availability for a specific service.

How can I schedule a tour?

You can use our Schedule a Tour page to prepare a visit request, call the business line at (360) 810-2158, or contact David directly at (302) 339-0579.

Where is House of David AFH located?

House of David AFH is located at 8737 Queens Ct, Olympia, WA 98513. You can use the Get Directions option in the Contact menu to open the location in Google Maps.
